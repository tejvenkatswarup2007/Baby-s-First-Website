How Mochi began

Scene 1

A very big egg

Long ago, in a meadow of marshmallow clouds, a very big egg sat very still. Inside, something tiny was humming a tune.

💥✨
Scene 2

Crack!

One sunny morning: crack, crack, CRACK! Out tumbled Mochi, wearing half the shell as a hat. “Rawr?” he said. It came out as a squeak.

⌨️🤏❓
Scene 3

The tiny-arm problem

Mochi wanted to type, paint and ring the doorbell. His arms did not reach. So he invented extra-big buttons, bouncy pads and tools you can use with a roar.

🍄🐌🐸☁️
Scene 4

Sprinkle Valley

Neighbours came to see: a shy snail, a sleepy cloud and a very loud frog. Mochi built a toy for each of them, and soon the whole valley was giggling.

🌙⭐
Scene 5

Still hatching

Every day Mochi learns something new. Tomorrow? A bigger bubble. Maybe a rocket. He's still hatching, and you're invited.
