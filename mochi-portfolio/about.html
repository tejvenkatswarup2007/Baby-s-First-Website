The 5-point sheet

Meet the baby

1

Name

Mochi, named after the squishy rice cake he's shaped like. Friends call him Mo.

2

What he is

A baby T-Rex, three egg-years old. Mint-green, round as a dumpling, with a pink blush, a tail that wags on its own and half an eggshell worn as a hat. Curious, giggly, clumsy and very kind. His roar sounds like a squeaky toy.

3

What he does

Junior Bubble Engineer at the Sprinkle Valley Workshop. He builds toys, games, sounds and tiny worlds that make people smile, and every button is extra big so tiny arms can reach it.

4

His story

Hatched from a giant egg in a meadow of marshmallow clouds. His arms were too short for the world's buttons, so he started inventing a new one. He's still hatching new ideas every day.

5

Tagline

“Tiny arms, giant ideas!”
